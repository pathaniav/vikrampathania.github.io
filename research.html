Selected Publications

Local Economic Impacts of Large-Scale Logistics Investments (with Co-author)

Journal of Policy Analysis and Management (JPAM). We study what happens to local employment, poverty, and income when large fulfillment centers open. Using county-level data and a difference-in-differences design, we find that these investments increase employment and incomes without significant displacement across other sectors.
