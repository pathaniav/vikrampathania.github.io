Vikram Pathania

Economist · Causal Inference · Marketplace Design & AI

I use economic theory, causal inference, and large-scale experiments to design safer, more efficient marketplaces and organizations. My training spans the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), the Indian Institute of Management (IIM), and a PhD in Economics from the University of California, Berkeley. I have held faculty positions at the London School of Economics and the University of Sussex, and I now work as a senior economist in big tech.

I am particularly interested in how platforms, incentives, regulation, and increasingly AI-driven decision systems shape outcomes for consumers, workers, and firms.
